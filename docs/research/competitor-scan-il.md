## Common visual language and copy clichés (to avoid)

- Copy openers: יוקרתי, מפנק, חלומי, הבוקר של החתונה, היום הכי מיוחד שלך, חוויה בלתי נשכחת, נוף עוצר נשימה, אווירה קסומה, יחס אישי. Almost nobody opens with a fact.
- Naming: English brand + Hebrew descriptor is the norm; "The House Of Brides" fits, so differentiate with the descriptor and the facts.
- Look: white/cream + gold or blush, script Latin logo, Hollywood mirror + jacuzzi + balcony hero shots, Instagram reel embeds.
- CTA: floating phone/WhatsApp button, "צרי קשר". No online booking, no cancellation policy anywhere.

## Implications for The House Of Brides

1. Lead with the facts nobody publishes as a scannable strip under each apartment hero: price from, hours, stations, max companions, parking, elevator, ממ"ד, sleeping, cancellation.
2. Public availability calendar + WhatsApp prefill ("בדקי זמינות ל-DD/MM בנתניה").
3. Two-location structure with city-SEO titles and a Jerusalem-hills page (Shoresh / Abu Gosh / Neve Ilan halls) where competition is thin.
4. Borrow Bride-day's "סדר יום" timeline and Hagit's calm tone; avoid gold/marble and the cliché openers.
5. A custom RTL build with real Hebrew typography will look visibly different from the Wix/WordPress/Shopix field.
6. Get listed on the directories; that is where page 1 actually is.
